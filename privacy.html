Privacy Policy

VidForge AI  ·  Last updated: July 22, 2026

1. Overview

VidForge AI is a desktop application that runs locally on your own computer. This policy explains what data the app handles and how, specifically in connection with TikTok account access.

2. What we access

When you connect your TikTok account, VidForge AI requests only the scopes needed to publish content on your behalf:

  • user.info.basic — your TikTok display name and avatar, shown in the app so you can tell accounts apart.
  • video.publish / video.upload — permission to upload and publish videos to your own TikTok account when you initiate a publishing job.

3. Where data is stored

All data — OAuth tokens, account info, generated videos, and job history — is stored locally on your own device, in an encrypted local database. VidForge AI does not operate a cloud server and does not transmit your TikTok data to any third party other than TikTok's own API, which is required to publish on your behalf.

4. What we never collect

VidForge AI never sees or stores your TikTok password. We do not sell, rent, or share your data with advertisers or other third parties.

5. Your control over your data

You can disconnect any TikTok account at any time from within the app, which removes its stored access token. Deleting the app's local data folder removes all locally stored information.

6. Third-party services

Publishing to TikTok is governed by TikTok's own Privacy Policy, in addition to this one.

7. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated from time to time. Continued use of the app after a change constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.

8. Contact

Questions about this policy can be directed to the app's maintainer.
